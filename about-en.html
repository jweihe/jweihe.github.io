About

Research interests: agentic LLMs, multimodal reasoning, graph learning, and agent evaluation.

Bio

I currently work on large language models in Beijing, with an interest in intelligent systems that can operate over long horizons, use tools, and improve through evaluation-driven feedback loops.

During my master’s study at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, my research focused on graph anomaly detection, graph out-of-distribution detection, and knowledge reasoning. The work led to publications at AAAI, ACM MM, and ICASSP, and I also contributed to open-source projects including SkillsBench and Harbor.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology. I am especially interested in methods that are reproducible, measurable, and useful in real systems.
