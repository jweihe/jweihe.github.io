About Me

I work on foundation models and agentic intelligence at ByteDance. My research focuses on agentic reinforcement learning, agent architectures, embodied intelligence, and physical intelligence.

I am especially interested in real-world, multi-turn agent tasks: how agents plan, use tools, adapt to feedback, and carry a task through to completion. I contribute to SkillsBench 1.9k+ and Harbor Adapters & Harbor-Index, built on the Harbor framework 6.8k+.

I completed my master’s research at VIPL, Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology.

Let’s connect and explore what’s next.

I enjoy exchanging ideas and working with people across research and engineering. I’m especially excited about foundation models, agentic RL, embodied and physical intelligence, and agents that learn through real-world interaction. Whether you’re working in these areas or simply curious, I’d love to hear from you.

SkillsBench: Benchmarking how well agent skills work across diverse tasks

Xiangyi Li, Wenbo Chen, Yimin Liu, et al. (including Junwei He)

A benchmark for testing whether procedural knowledge packaged as reusable skills translates into real agent capability. Curated tasks and deterministic verifiers compare agents with and without skills, separating skill quality from an agent’s ability to discover and apply them. Used in frontier-model evaluations.

Harbor Adapters and Harbor-Index: Infrastructure and a Curated Meta-Dataset for Large-Scale Agentic Evaluation

Lin Shi, Haowei Lin, Zixuan Zhu, Xiaoyue Zhou, et al. (including Junwei He)
