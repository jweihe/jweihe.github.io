About Me

I work on foundation models and agentic intelligence at ByteDance. My research spans reinforcement learning, credit assignment, and reward design, with the goal of building agents that turn interaction into reliable reasoning and action.

I contribute to SkillsBench and Harbor Adapters & Harbor-Index, two open-source efforts advancing how we evaluate and improve general-purpose agents. Both were accepted to NeurIPS 2026 (E&B Track). Together, they connect reusable agent skills, diverse real-world tasks, and reproducible evaluation at scale.

I completed my master’s research at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology.

I welcome research discussions and collaborations.

I am especially excited to work with people exploring foundation models, agentic RL, and agent evaluation. If you have an idea, a difficult problem, or a project we could build together, let’s talk ↗.
