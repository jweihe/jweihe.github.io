About Me

I work on large language model algorithms at ByteDance, with a focus on agents that reason over multi-step tasks, use tools, and learn from interaction and feedback.

My recent research connects agentic reinforcement learning, credit assignment, reward design, and evaluation. I am interested in turning reliable feedback into better reasoning and more capable agents.

Previously, I completed my master’s research at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, working on graph learning and knowledge reasoning. I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology.

I welcome research discussions and collaborations. Feel free to reach out.
