About Me

I work on foundation models and agentic intelligence at ByteDance. My research focuses on agentic reinforcement learning, agent architectures, embodied intelligence, and physical intelligence.

I am especially interested in real-world, multi-turn agent tasks: how agents plan, use tools, adapt to feedback, and carry a task through to completion. I contribute to SkillsBench 1.9k+ and Harbor Adapters & Harbor-Index, built on the Harbor framework 6.8k+.

I completed my master’s research at VIPL, Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology.

Let’s connect!

I enjoy exchanging ideas and working with people across research and engineering.
